What Is an Aerospace Vehicle?

Aerospace vehicles operate in the atmosphere, beyond it, or across both environments.

What Is an Aerospace Vehicle? is best understood by examining its operating environment, source of lift or thrust, major subsystems, mission profile and physical limits.

Classification matters to safety engineering because the same hazard does not have the same consequence or control strategy across different vehicle architectures. Vehicle configuration determines which energy sources, control functions, structural loads, environmental stresses and human interactions must be controlled.

A safety engineer should therefore understand the vehicle before analysing its hazards: what keeps it airborne or in orbit, what provides propulsion, how it is controlled, what can fail, who or what is exposed, and which barriers are available.
